**PRICE_LIST** holds the 56 products/packs transcribed from the Renova Pharmaceuticals list effective April 2026. Its columns are Sl_No, Product_SKU, Product_Name, Pack, MRP, PTS, PTR, Tax, Scheme, Paid_Packs, Free_Packs, Net_Price, Effective_From, Manufacturer, Mapping_Status, Notes and Source. Net_Price is a sheet formula rounded to two decimals: **PTR × paid packs ÷ (paid + free packs), before tax**. Tax is stored as a fraction (0.05 for 5%), Scheme as text, and Effective_From as YYYY-MM. The parentheses around PTS in the source denote positive listed prices. Prices use the pack printed in the source; no per-tablet or carton conversion is inferred.

Product cards, product details and doctor-linked products show MRP, PTR, Net Price, PTS, tax, scheme and the effective month. **Product explorer → Price list** includes all 56 entries and searches by product, SKU or pack, independently of sales filters. Matching uses the exact canonical SKU: 51 entries link to existing products. ANECHEK XT DROPS 30ML, DIK LINIMENT 50gm, MYLINOVA TAB, REVIT GOLD DROPS and SAP A 10'S have separate price-only SKUs; the 50gm liniment and 10'S SAP A prices are not applied to existing 30ML and 20'S products. Mapping and pack differences are recorded in Notes. Prices remain separate from stock valuations, sales estimates and POB quantities.

Product explorer adds **Momentum & actions** alongside Stock cover. Sales momentum compares average daily unit sales in the three calendar months ending at To against the preceding three months, using actual calendar-day counts. Rising/Falling are beyond ±10%; Stable is within that band. Started/resumed, Stopped and confirmed No sales have explicit categories, and missing full statements, missing product observations or negative adjustments remain Insufficient data. Aliases aggregate by canonical SKU and every historically tracked agency within the selected agency/brand/inclusion scope must have comparable coverage. The From filter continues to scope period sales and movement status; the signal windows are labelled separately.

Clickable momentum and review-category cards filter the catalogue, action matrix and agency visuals together with search, movement and cover filters. The matrix plots finite three-month stock cover against daily sales-pace growth, with 30/90-day and ±10% reference lines. Started/resumed sales and zero baselines never get invented percentage growth, and non-moving stock never gets infinite cover; unplotted products remain in the exact table and review groups. Low cover with rising or started/resumed sales triggers Replenishment review; zero stock with recent sales triggers Stockout review; high cover with falling/stopped sales or confirmed non-moving stock triggers Excess-stock review. Other falling/stopped products go to Demand review. These are review signals at the statement date, not automatic orders.

Stock value by cover category stacks actual closing inventory valuations by agency, with each agency classified separately. A matching tracked product without a full-month closing valuation makes that agency's entire stack unavailable rather than showing an incomplete total; the exact table retains the available agencies and unavailable combined totals. Chart segments and agency-value cells focus that agency and cover category. Product cards and detail pages show momentum and review badges. Explorer filters and analysis tabs survive product drill-down and Back. No Apps Script update is required. The publisher includes all new signal files and checks their dependencies before upload.

Daily reports brings the dfr2 morning/evening workflow into this app. Open **Daily reports** in the main navigation, select the date and work place, and copy a ready-to-send morning message. Enter doctors visited, chemists visited, new conversions and POB once in Evening; the preview and cumulative totals update immediately. Copying saves pending entries first. Records autosave after one second, remain available when navigating between MedMetric pages, and past dates are read-only. Asia/Kolkata dates roll over automatically while today's report is open. Reporting weeks are days 1–7, 8–14, 15–21 and 22–month-end. Doctors and chemists accumulate monthly with opening balances; POB can accumulate monthly or continuously.

Daily report data is stored only in this browser under `medmetric-daily-reports-v1`, independently of the Google Sheets sales and doctor/pharmacy data. These daily counts and rupee POB amounts are manually entered report totals; they are not inferred from doctor profiles, unit-based POB bookings, or agency sales. New records start at zero. Settings & backup holds the name/HQ, monthly opening balances and JSON export/import; existing dfr2 backups are supported and validated before a preview and explicit restore. A single storage write keeps each save atomic, and storage failures remain visibly unsaved with retry and backup actions. Download a backup to move reports between browsers or devices; clearing browser storage removes the local copy. Dark/light appearance applies to the daily-report workspace. No Apps Script deployment change is required for this feature.

Product rankings include estimated sales value (with a unit-sales alternative) and zero-sale / low-sale remaining stock. Global month, agency, brand and inclusion filters apply to both, with local search, Top 5/10/20, product/agency scope, sales threshold, minimum stock and recorded age controls. Every chart includes exact table values, brief calculated insights and product drill-down. The source has product sale units and closing stock valuation, but no product revenue: the value ranking is explicitly an estimate, calculated per source row as units sold × closing value / closing units. Sold-out products with no valuation remain unpriced; switch to units to see them. Classification requires full monthly statements and observations across the entire selected period; negative sale adjustments and unknown coverage do not become zero-sale products. Closing stock is taken only from the selected end month. Aliases aggregate before classification, and product totals require every historically tracked agency in the filtered scope. No backend update is needed.

The medrep product catalogue and prescribing-product text are not imported: selectors use PRODUCT_CONFIG names and Your_SKU only. Secondary revenue targets count all statement sales, including direct agency connections. Unit targets use logged prescription or booked POB quantities separately. Saves validate foreign IDs, dates, quantities and fulfilment status, escape formula-like notes, prevent stale edits and make retries idempotent. These features require the updated existing Code.gs deployment. The shared schema is regenerated into Code.gs before npm test and npm run build.

The browser loads one snapshot and saves it locally. Filters, charts, source-row drilldowns and page reloads reuse that snapshot until Refresh data is clicked. A failed refresh preserves the previous snapshot. If browser storage is blocked or cleared, the next page load must fetch data again. The server shares concurrent reads and caches successful snapshots for one minute; Refresh data bypasses that cache. No Apps Script update is required for these frontend changes.

Edit charts in components/ and calculations in lib/analytics.js. Double-click Commit-and-Push.cmd to publish app changes. The publisher uses this folder directly. Backend changes require updating Code.gs in the existing Apps Script project and its existing deployment.

The dashboard opens directly, without login. Anyone with the website URL can read the displayed sales data. Vercel uses APPS_SCRIPT_URL and MEDMETRIC_BACKEND_SECRET; Apps Script stores the same backend secret as a Script Property. The backend secret stays on the servers. Never commit .env.local.

Code.gs accepts signed, time-limited POST requests. Sales reads use the three fixed source tabs plus the optional PRICE_LIST; field reads use DOCTORS, PHARMACIES, DOCTOR_PRODUCTS, POB_ACTIVITY, FIELD_OPTIONS, the existing product catalogue and PRICE_LIST. Validated writes are restricted to doctors, product links and POBs, with pharmacy creation handled only as part of a doctor save under the same script lock. Its public GET response contains only a service status. The web app deployment runs as its owner; public endpoint reachability does not bypass request authentication.

Use Node.js 22 or newer. npm ci, npm test, npm run build. No Google OAuth client ID or client secret is used.

Stock-cover classification uses closing units at the selected To month divided by average daily unit sales over the three calendar months ending at To (actual calendar days, including leap years). Fast is below 30 days, Medium 30–90, Slow above 90–180, Very slow above 180. Positive stock with confirmed zero baseline sales is Non-moving, confirmed zero closing stock is Out of stock, and missing/full-month/negative-adjustment problems remain Insufficient data. Stock age is separate and never inferred from cover. Explorer category filters and badges, selected-product summaries and agency cover charts share these rules. Combined coverage is calculated from combined stock and units, not an average of agency ratios. From month still scopes period sales and movement checks; the stock-cover baseline is always explicitly labelled.
