# MedMetric

Product rankings include estimated sales value (with a unit-sales alternative) and zero-sale / low-sale remaining stock. Global month, agency, brand and inclusion filters apply to both, with local search, Top 5/10/20, product/agency scope, sales threshold, minimum stock and recorded age controls. Every chart includes exact table values, brief calculated insights and product drill-down. The source has product sale units and closing stock valuation, but no product revenue: the value ranking is explicitly an estimate, calculated per source row as units sold × closing value / closing units. Sold-out products with no valuation remain unpriced; switch to units to see them. Classification requires full monthly statements and observations across the entire selected period; negative sale adjustments and unknown coverage do not become zero-sale products. Closing stock is taken only from the selected end month. Aliases aggregate before classification, and product totals require every historically tracked agency in the filtered scope. No backend update is needed.

One project: Next.js on Vercel, with Google Sheets connected through apps-script/Code.gs.

Doctors & pharmacies adds doctor profiles, manually logged prescriptions, latest dated pharmacy stock checks, follow-ups, product relationships, POBs and targets. It reads the eight tracking tabs once and stores the snapshot in this browser; navigation, filters and page reloads reuse it until Refresh data. Successful saves update the stored snapshot; failed refreshes preserve it. Saves update the saved entry immediately and preserve unsuccessful entries for retry. Doctor visits are excluded. The medrep product catalogue and prescribing-product text are not imported: selectors use PRODUCT_CONFIG names and Your_SKU only. Public viewing and editing were explicitly requested; master lists and financial source tabs cannot be modified through the activity API.

Unknown prescription dates and quantities remain blank. Prescriptions and POBs do not decrement observed stock or add agency revenue. Stock is shared at pharmacy level, separated by product and quantity unit, with a configurable recheck threshold. Secondary revenue targets count all statement sales, including direct agency connections. Unit targets use logged prescription or booked POB quantities separately. Saves validate foreign IDs, dates, quantities and fulfilment status, escape formula-like notes, prevent stale edits and make retries idempotent. These features require the updated existing Code.gs deployment. The shared schema is regenerated into Code.gs before npm test and npm run build.

The report workspace has dedicated Overview, Sales rankings, Stock watch, Product explorer and Data quality pages. Desktop navigation and report slicers stay visible while scrolling; mobile uses a compact sticky filter summary that expands in place. Sales and stock charts have independent month/agency/brand slicers with Latest, 3 months and All time shortcuts. Chart overrides are visibly labelled and can reset to current report filters. Drilling into a product carries the chart period and agency, and Back restores the originating report, its filter selection and scroll position. Ranking controls survive opening a product. Product explorer opens with stock-cover category counts and comparable agency sales/stock bars linked to its searchable catalogue. Selecting a status filters the agency visual and product list; catalogue search and status survive a product drill-down. Selected products show monthly sales and stock lines directly below their primary metrics in Analysis & charts. Agency comparison merges grouped sales/restocking bars and the exact agency table through Chart/Table. Additional growth metrics and monthly receipt detail are expandable to keep the main analysis focused. Overview pairs its financial trend with selected-period agency sales contribution instead of a second monthly sales chart. The Insights visibility control applies throughout. Selecting a product from a filter, catalog, ranking or inventory row opens a dedicated product analysis page. It shows that product's unit sales, receipts, closing stock, monthly sales by agency, agency comparisons, three-month trends and stock allocation signals. Product context survives month and agency changes, including selections with no observations. Financial summaries remain in Overview; product insights use only that product's observations, with full-month coverage required for growth comparisons.

Product charts show monthly Restocked vs Sold bars separately for each agency, plus month-end stock unit lines comparing agencies. Every chart has top-right Chart/Table buttons for its exact plotted values. Missing observations stay gaps or —; actual zero values remain zero. Chart and month-table clicks open the corresponding source rows, scoped to the agency when applicable.

Agency comparison bars share one value scale. Product totals require observations for each selected agency and month, and agency-share insights require comparable full-month coverage. Idle-product insights aggregate aliases by SKU first. Three-month patterns stay within the chart's displayed period. Each product chart has a takeaway using its plotted values, with partial coverage visible in tooltips and tables. The loaded timestamp includes its date so saved snapshots remain identifiable.

Product performance cards include clickable per-agency values and percentages where coverage permits. Additional cards show latest-month sales growth, restocked units minus sold units, and estimated stock cover (closing units divided by latest full-month unit sales). Stock cover is an estimate at the last observed sales pace; zero or missing sales never produce infinite cover. The receipt/sales gap is not presented as a change in closing stock. Date scopes and unavailable-data reasons are shown with each metric.

The browser loads one snapshot and saves it locally. Filters, charts, source-row drilldowns and page reloads reuse that snapshot until Refresh data is clicked. A failed refresh preserves the previous snapshot. If browser storage is blocked or cleared, the next page load must fetch data again. The server shares concurrent reads and caches successful snapshots for one minute; Refresh data bypasses that cache. No Apps Script update is required for these frontend changes.

Edit charts in components/ and calculations in lib/analytics.js. Double-click Commit-and-Push.cmd to publish app changes. The publisher uses this folder directly. Backend changes require updating Code.gs in the existing Apps Script project and its existing deployment.

The dashboard opens directly, without login. Anyone with the website URL can read the displayed sales data. Vercel uses APPS_SCRIPT_URL and MEDMETRIC_BACKEND_SECRET; Apps Script stores the same backend secret as a Script Property. The backend secret stays on the servers. Never commit .env.local.

Code.gs accepts signed, time-limited POST requests. Sales reads still use the three fixed source tabs; field reads use the eight tracking tabs and the existing product catalogue. Validated writes are restricted to six activity/relationship/target tabs and run under a script lock. Its public GET response contains only a service status. The web app deployment runs as its owner; public endpoint reachability does not bypass request authentication.

Use Node.js 22 or newer. npm ci, npm test, npm run build. No Google OAuth client ID or client secret is used.

Stock-cover classification uses closing units at the selected To month divided by average daily unit sales over the three calendar months ending at To (actual calendar days, including leap years). Fast is below 30 days, Medium 30–90, Slow above 90–180, Very slow above 180. Positive stock with confirmed zero baseline sales is Non-moving, confirmed zero closing stock is Out of stock, and missing/full-month/negative-adjustment problems remain Insufficient data. Stock age is separate and never inferred from cover. Explorer category filters and badges, selected-product summaries and agency cover charts share these rules. Combined coverage is calculated from combined stock and units, not an average of agency ratios. From month still scopes period sales and movement checks; the stock-cover baseline is always explicitly labelled.
